## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for a cutting-edge artificial intelligence platform. The brand personality is authoritative yet approachable, futuristic, and relentlessly innovative. It evokes a sense of infinite computational intelligence, precision, and seamless human-AI collaboration.

The chosen design style merges **Glassmorphism** and **High-Contrast Dark Mode**, characterized by translucent surfaces, vibrant background blurs, deep atmospheric dark tones, and glowing electric accents that simulate advanced neural networks.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by a deep dark slate base (`#0b0f19`) to reduce eye strain during intensive data analysis and prompt engineering. A rich, cosmic purple (`#7c3aed`) defines the secondary focal points, while an electric blue (`#3b82f6`) acts as the high-energy accent for active states, primary actions, and neon highlights. Neutral slate tones provide structural hierarchy without breaking the immersive dark environment.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Inter** to maintain an ultra-clean, utilitarian, yet sophisticated aesthetic. For viewports under 768px, scale down `headline-xl` to 36px and `headline-lg` to 28px to maintain layout integrity. Ensure font smoothing is enforced globally (`-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased`) for the crisp rendering required in dense data-driven interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

A **fluid grid** system is utilized, adapting dynamically to workspace resizing. The canvas uses a 12-column structure with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous outer margins to frame complex dashboard modules. 

On mobile devices, collapse sidebars into slide-over drawers, reduce outer margins to `1rem`, and stack full-width containers vertically.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is achieved primarily through **Glassmorphism and ambient glows** rather than traditional hard drop shadows. Surfaces use semi-transparent dark slate fills (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.75)`) paired with high-blur backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). 

Borders act as subtle light traps: use 1px inner strokes featuring gradient fades from the electric blue accent to transparent, creating a self-illuminating edge effect on active or hovered containers.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape language (`rounded-md` base of `0.5rem`, `rounded-lg` of `1rem`, and `rounded-xl` of `1.5rem`). This softens the clinical nature of dark-mode technical dashboards, making interactive touchpoints feel tactile, modern, and inviting. Pill shapes (`3`) are reserved exclusively for status indicators, AI confidence tags, and primary action badges.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary buttons feature a rich purple-to-electric-blue gradient background with a soft outer glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.4)`). Secondary buttons use a glassmorphic surface with a subtle 1px neutral border and a white text highlight. Transitions must be swift and smooth (150ms ease-out).

### Chips & Tags
Compact, pill-shaped elements utilized for model selection (e.g., "GPT-4", "Claude 3.5") or operational tags. Inactive chips use low-opacity neutral fills; active chips glow with electric blue borders and tinted backgrounds.

### Lists
Data streams, chat histories, and prompt logs are displayed in structured rows with generous vertical padding, separated by 1px hairline dividers using semi-transparent neutral slates. Hover states subtly elevate the background tone.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
Custom geometric controls featuring sharp square (checkbox) and circular (radio) indicators. Checked states fill with the electric blue accent and display a faint internal bloom.

### Input Fields
Prompt bars and text inputs are designed as high-depth glassmorphic wells. They feature a dark translucent fill, rounded corners, and focus states that trigger an animated electric blue border glow and expanded outer shadow.

### Cards
Container elements for AI outputs, analytics widgets, and agent configurations. Cards utilize frosted glass surfaces, rounded corners (`rounded-lg`), and subtle glowing edge highlights to establish a futuristic floating aesthetic.

### Additional Components: AI Prompt Bar & Output Stream
The central interaction model requires a floating bottom prompt bar with multi-modal attachment chips, voice input triggers, and an instant execution button. Output streams should support markdown formatting, syntax-highlighted code blocks with one-click copy actions, and streaming cursor animations.
